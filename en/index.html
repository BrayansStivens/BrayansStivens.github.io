Pitangus

$ pitangus --about
pitangusv0.15 · beta · AGPL-3.0

Spot it. Fix it. Prove it.

Security tools warn a lot and prove little. Pitangus is self-hosted, open-source AppSec: it prioritizes what is exploitable, tells you how to fix it and confirms the fix worked, without sending your code anywhere. I designed it and built it with AI agents; it is now published by Arodium, the company I co-founded.

  1. 01find

    Seven open-source engines and 66 custom rules, including rules for the typical mistakes in AI-written code.

  2. 02prioritize

    Every advisory is checked against CISA KEV and EPSS.

  3. 03fix

    The exact command for your package manager.

  4. 04verify

    Reverify, plus pull request checks that can block the merge.

  5. 05watch

    Rescans on every change and alerts to Slack, Teams or a webhook.

  6. 06prove

    SOC 2 and ISO 27001 evidence, SARIF and PDF reports.

A supply chain you can check: digest-pinned images, signed releases and a public OpenSSF Scorecard.
